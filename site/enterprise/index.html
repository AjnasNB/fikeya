The enterprise workflow

Policy follows the agent from model selection to verified outcome.

The model never becomes the authority. A central decision is enforced again at the endpoint, and an approval proves permission only. Post-execution evidence proves what actually happened.

  1. 01
    Identify

    Bind tenant, authenticated human, workload, endpoint, project, purpose, and data class.

  2. 02
    Route

    Choose from approved providers and models under residency, retention, quality, cost, and availability rules.

  3. 03
    Budget

    Apply request, session, user, team, project, and tenant token or cost ceilings.

  4. 04
    Evaluate

    Return allow, deny, or review with reasons and obligations from a versioned policy.

  5. 05
    Approve

    Pause for an authenticated exact-call decision when the action is consequential.

  6. 06
    Execute

    Run through the local broker or sandbox with filesystem, network, origin, and runtime limits.

  7. 07
    Verify

    Collect exit state, affected paths, hashes, tests, usage, and Qarinah references.

  8. 08
    Export

    Deliver configured content-bounded records to external audit, SIEM, and retention systems.

Deployment boundary

Local execution, central policy, explicit offline behavior.

Fikeya remains the endpoint enforcement point. Maqam supplies the registered-tool governance component. Signed-policy expiry and offline behavior must be demonstrated as part of deployment acceptance.

Identity acceptanceOIDC or SAML, SCIM, user and workload identity
Isolation acceptanceTenant-scoped data, keys, policies, queues, and audit
Reliability acceptanceIdempotency, retries, dead letters, reconciliation, and recovery drills
Operations acceptanceLogs, metrics, traces, alerts, SIEM acknowledgement, and rollout health

Honest readiness

A design-partner system, not a finished compliance claim.

The public Fikeya beta already supplies local provider adapters, bounded cited context, one-use approvals, root-bound tools, and receipts. Production enterprise claims require verified SSO, tenant isolation, KMS, workload identity, resilient queues, external audit delivery, load and failover tests, penetration testing, and recovery exercises.
